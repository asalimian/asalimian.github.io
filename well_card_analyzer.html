Runs client-side only — no data leaves your browser. Revenue estimates use annual average WTI (oil) and Henry Hub (gas) benchmark spot prices, not this well's actual sale price, and exclude taxes, royalties, and operating costs. Produced water is treated as a disposal item, not revenue. Oil prices cover 1986–2025 (WTI Cushing, EIA); gas prices cover 1997–2025 (Henry Hub, EIA). Years outside these ranges use the nearest available year's price.
