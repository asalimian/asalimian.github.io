The Loan Payoff Calculator

This tool can help you figure out how best to pay off your loan, and figure out if you have any "good debt"

Auto-save can be enabled in the settings menu, and all data is stored in your device (not on this server), or you can copy out the JSON string so that you can keep your own back-up.

The Inputs

Principle
How much you have right now.

Rate
How much your annual loan rate is. To annualize your investment return use ln(1+total return)/years.

Min payment
Used to calculate your repayment period.

Income
How much income you have available for repaying loans or investing.

Loan / Savings split
How to allocate your discretionary income.

The Outputs

Payment
How much of your income is allocated to paying back your loan or saving.

Schedule Years
Standard repayment period.

Years
When you'll be done repaying your loan; for the right column, how many years your full income goes to saving.
